Write a short follow-up email for a student who has not had a reply to an
earlier internship enquiry.

# CRITICAL

This is follow-up number {followup_number} of a maximum of 3.
It was sent {days_since} days after the original email.

Do NOT repeat the original email. They already have it. A follow-up that
restates the whole message is just noise, and it reads as automated.

Keep it SHORT. {length_guidance}

# THE ORIGINAL EMAIL THEY RECEIVED

Subject: {original_subject}

{original_body}

# THE STUDENT

{student_profile}

# WHAT THIS FOLLOW-UP SHOULD DO

{followup_purpose}

# RULES

- Reference the earlier email briefly, without quoting it.
- Add something small and useful, or ask a simpler question than before.
  A follow-up that only says "just checking in" wastes their time.
- No guilt, no pressure, no "I have not heard back from you".
- Do not imply they were rude or slow. They are busy.
- Never invent anything about the organisation.
- Do not restate the student's full skill list. One short phrase at most.
- No flattery. Do not use: passionate, thrilled, delighted, excited,
  leverage, esteemed, renowned, cutting-edge, world-class.
- No exclamation marks.
- Do not sign off with the student's name -- a signature block is added
  automatically afterwards.
- Address it the same way as the original: a team, never an invented
  person's name.

Subject line: reply-style, referencing the original thread. For example
"Re: {original_subject}". Keep it recognisable so they can connect it to
the first email.

# OUTPUT

Return ONLY a JSON object. No markdown fences, no commentary.

{{
  "subject": "the subject line",
  "body": "the email body, with \\n between paragraphs, ending at the closing line"
}}
