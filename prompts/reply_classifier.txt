Classify a reply that an organisation sent to a student's internship or
industrial attachment enquiry.

# THE ORIGINAL ENQUIRY

The student wrote to {company_name} asking whether they would consider
an intern or industrial attachment placement.

Subject: {original_subject}

# THEIR REPLY

From: {from_address}
Subject: {reply_subject}

{reply_body}

# CLASSIFY IT

Choose exactly one:

- POSITIVE   -- interest, encouragement, or an invitation to continue.
                They want to hear more, or say a placement is possible.
- NEGATIVE   -- a clear no. No openings, not hiring interns, or a
                decline. A polite rejection is still NEGATIVE.
- REFERRAL   -- they point to a different person, department, or
                process. Includes "apply through our portal" and
                "contact our HR team instead".
- CV_REQUEST -- they ask for a CV, transcript, portfolio, or an
                application form.
- INTERVIEW  -- they propose a call, meeting, interview, or ask about
                availability to speak.
- OTHER      -- anything that fits none of the above, including
                acknowledgements that say nothing yet.

If a reply does more than one of these, choose the one that determines
what the student must DO next. An invitation to interview outranks a
request for a CV; a CV request outranks a general positive tone.

# WHAT YOU MUST NOT DO

Do not invent details. If they did not name a date, a person, or a role,
do not supply one. Quote or paraphrase only what the reply actually says.

Do not be optimistic. A polite brush-off is NEGATIVE, not POSITIVE. Read
what they mean, not what the student would like them to mean.

# NEXT ACTION

Say plainly what the student should do next, in a few words. For example:
"Send CV", "Reply with availability", "Apply through their portal",
"Contact the referred person", "Wait -- they will be in touch",
"No further action", "Draft a reply".

If no action is needed, say so. Do not invent busywork.

# OUTPUT

Return ONLY a JSON object. No markdown fences, no commentary.

{{
  "classification": "POSITIVE" | "NEGATIVE" | "REFERRAL" | "CV_REQUEST" | "INTERVIEW" | "OTHER",
  "confidence": 0.0 to 1.0,
  "summary": "one or two factual sentences on what they actually said",
  "next_action": "a few words on what the student should do",
  "referred_to": "the person, team or URL they pointed to, or UNKNOWN",
  "deadline_mentioned": "any date or deadline they named, or UNKNOWN"
}}
